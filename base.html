DESIGN RULES

Warehouse first. Theme park never.

The aesthetic should feel cohesive, functional, and intentionally restrained.

VOLUME

Keep it open

Protect long sightlines, tall ceilings, circulation, and flexible floor space.

MATERIAL

Industrial restraint

Concrete, dark metal, wood accents, matte finishes, acoustic treatment, and practical surfaces.

LIGHT

Dark, not dim

Low ambient mood with serious task lighting for cooking, maintenance, computing, and safety.

CULTURE

Japanese influence

Selected art, typography, storage discipline, and dedicated anime spaces integrated into the wider architecture.

GATES BEFORE PURCHASE

The dream gets paperwork.

A warehouse conversion only becomes a real project after these gates are understood for a specific property and municipality.

Residency + financing position

Know what can legally and realistically be rented, purchased, financed, or operated.

Gate 01

Use + zoning feasibility

Confirm whether residential or mixed live/work use is possible before falling in love with the shell.

Gate 02

Building condition

Structure, roof, seismic considerations, moisture, hazardous materials, access, and repair burden.

Gate 03

Utilities + life safety

Electrical capacity, plumbing, drainage, ventilation, insulation, fire separation, exits, alarms, and occupancy requirements.

Gate 04

Conversion budget

Only after real quotes exist does the site get a public funding target for E.V. Base.
