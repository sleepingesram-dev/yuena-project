ORDER OF OPERATIONS

Skill before speed.

The project is focused on becoming a capable rider before prioritizing performance or modifications.

01 / TRAIN

Rider course

Structured instruction, low-speed control, braking, hazard awareness, and licensing requirements.

02 / PROTECT

Real gear

Helmet, gloves, jacket, riding pants, boots, and visibility choices before optional bike cosmetics.

03 / BUY

First motorcycle

A manageable, reliable motorcycle chosen for condition, fit, and intended use.

04 / MAINTAIN

Know the machine

Inspection habits, tires, chain, fluids, brakes, battery, and the ability to recognize when something is wrong.

WHERE IT GOES

From first ride to Japan.

The motorcycle is also part of the larger story: eventually understanding the licensing, ownership, parking, insurance, maintenance, and road culture needed to ride safely in Japan.

FUNDING PRINCIPLE

Safety is not the boring part of the budget.

  • Training and protective gear come before modifications.
  • Maintenance reserve stays separate from cosmetic spending.
  • Every major purchase can be documented with why it was chosen.
  • Future Japan-specific purchases wait until the relocation path is concrete.
