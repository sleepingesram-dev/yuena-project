PAYMENTS / PRIVACY

Payments are live through Stripe.

Direct project support opens Stripe-hosted Checkout. Yuena Core also has a Ko-fi option for one-time support. This website itself never receives or stores your card number or payment credentials.

Support is optional, non-charitable, and not tax-deductible. It does not purchase goods or services. The Yuena Core public total can include both direct Stripe support and Ko-fi support after reconciliation, so counters may lag behind completed payments.
