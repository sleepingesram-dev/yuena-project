WHAT SUPPORT MEANS

Acceleration, not dependency.

The move should still work without strangers funding it. Support helps reach well-defined milestones sooner and gives followers a concrete piece of the journey to back.

NON-NEGOTIABLE

Japan is the destination. Stability is the requirement.

  • No warehouse purchase before the legal, income, and location picture is clear.
  • No counting public support as guaranteed future income.
  • No sacrificing the emergency reserve for aesthetics.
  • Every major milestone gets documented when it becomes real.
